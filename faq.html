Help for families

Frequently asked questions

Find answers about admissions, school stages, fees, visits and contacting Columbus Model High School. For details that may change, please confirm directly with the school.

Do you publish fee information online?

Fee details are not published online. Please call the school at +91 90002 32197 or +91 90304 74447.

Which documents are required for admission?

Please call the school for the current document list. Do not send identity numbers through this website form.

Which grades does the school offer?

The website describes classes from pre-primary through Grade 10. Contact the school to confirm availability for a particular class.

Which curriculum does the school follow?

Columbus Model High School follows the Telangana State Board curriculum.

How can I ask about admission availability?

Call the admissions team at +91 90002 32197 or use the enquiry form.

How do I arrange a campus visit?

Please call the school in advance at +91 90002 32197 to discuss a suitable visit time.

Where is the school located?

Columbus Model High School is at Plot No: 5, Opp. Miyapur Depot, Mayuri Nagar, Miyapur, Hyderabad – 500049, Telangana.

What are the school office hours?

The listed school hours are Monday to Saturday, 8:30 AM to 3:30 PM. Call to confirm office availability before visiting.

How can I contact the school?

Call +91 90002 32197, email enquiry@columbusmodelhighschool.com, or use the contact section.

Can I enquire about primary classes?

The primary-years page covers pre-primary through Grade 5. Contact the school to confirm current availability and details.

Can I enquire about middle school?

The middle-school page covers Grades 6–8. Contact the school to confirm current availability and details.

Can I enquire about high school?

The high-school page covers Grades 9–10. Contact the school for current subject, assessment and admission information.

Can I apply online?

This website supports an enquiry through WhatsApp; it does not accept a complete online application or document uploads. Contact the school for next steps.

Does the website collect or store enquiry form submissions?

No. The form opens WhatsApp with a message for you to review and send. This website does not store or email submissions.

How can I find the campus?

Use the Google Maps directions or call the school for help.

How can I ask about school events?

Contact the school directly or check its official social channels for updates. Events will be listed on this website when dates are confirmed.

Still have a question?

Call +91 90002 32197 or email the school. You can also send an admissions enquiry.
