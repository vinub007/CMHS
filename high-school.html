High School

Grades 9–10 follow the Telangana SSC pathway, helping students deepen their learning and prepare for the Class 10 board examinations.

Learning in Grades 9–10

Telangana SSC Curriculum

Study in the Telangana State Board system, with Grade 10 culminating in the SSC public examinations.

Focused preparation

Build understanding across the curriculum and develop consistent study habits for board assessments. Contact the school for current subject and exam guidance.

Technology and enrichment

The school highlights technology-enabled classrooms, STEM and robotics, sports, arts, music, drama, MUN and debate. Ask which activities are available in Grades 9–10.

Guidance for the next step

The school’s published information emphasizes academic guidance and student support. Families can contact the school to discuss subject choices, study support and the transition after Grade 10.

Learning beyond the classroom

CMHS also highlights sports and athletics, arts, music and drama, robotics and STEM clubs, MUN and debate as part of student life. Contact the school to confirm current schedules and availability for high-school students.
