Accessibility

Columbus Model High School aims to make this website usable by as many people as possible. The site provides keyboard-operable navigation, a skip link, labelled forms, visible focus indicators, and text alternatives for informative images.

We are working toward WCAG 2.2 Level AA. This statement is not a certification; some content or third-party services may not yet meet that target.

Need help?

If you have difficulty accessing information on this site, contact the school at enquiry@columbusmodelhighschool.com or +91 90002 32197.
