Vision, mission & chairman’s message

Our vision

What we believe

Every child deserves the opportunity to learn, discover their strengths and prepare for the future while staying connected to the values that shape a caring and responsible society.

Our mission

Our mission is to make meaningful, quality learning accessible and to support each student’s academic, personal and social growth. We aim to:

  • Build strong foundations in knowledge, communication and independent thinking.
  • Use modern technology purposefully to support learning, creativity and readiness for a changing world.
  • Nurture respect, integrity, curiosity, compassion and pride in India’s diverse cultural values.
  • Encourage students to take responsibility, work with others and contribute positively to their communities.
  • Work in partnership with families and educators to help every learner progress with confidence.

A message from our Chairman

Dear parents, students and well-wishers,

Education is one of the most meaningful ways we can prepare the next generation for the future. At Columbus Model High School, we aspire to provide every child with quality learning, encouragement and the opportunity to discover their potential.

As technology continues to shape the world around us, we believe it should be used thoughtfully to enrich learning. At the same time, our students should remain grounded in the Indian values of respect, integrity, compassion and responsibility.

We are grateful to our families and educators for being partners in this journey. Together, let us help our children grow into confident, curious and caring citizens who can contribute to India’s future.

With warm regards,

Mr. PeddannaChairman, Columbus Model High School
