Information collected

The enquiry forms ask for a parent or contact name, Indian mobile number and (for admission enquiries) student name and grade. The contact form also accepts an optional email address and a message. The forms do not ask for Aadhaar numbers or other identity numbers.

In the current static website, submitting a form opens a pre-filled WhatsApp message for you to review. The information is sent to WhatsApp only if you choose to send it there. This website does not currently save form submissions or email them to the school. WhatsApp processes information under its own privacy terms.

Purpose and consent

The requested details are used to respond to admission and general enquiries. Each form has an unchecked consent box; do not submit the message if you do not agree. You may contact the school directly by phone instead.

Retention

This website does not retain form submissions. The school has proposed a 12-month retention period for enquiries handled by a future server-side form service; this period is not yet implemented and must be confirmed by the school before that service goes live.

Cookies and preferences

This site does not currently load analytics or advertising tools. Your optional-cookie choice is stored in your browser's local storage so the preference banner can remember your choice. Use “Cookie settings” in the website footer to reopen the preference controls.

Your rights and questions

You may ask the school to provide, correct or delete personal information that you have sent to it, or withdraw consent for future use. Contact the school at enquiry@columbusmodelhighschool.com or call +91 90002 32197.

Grievance contact

For privacy concerns, contact the school at enquiry@columbusmodelhighschool.com or write to Columbus Model High School, Plot No: 5, Opp. Miyapur Depot, Mayuri Nagar, Miyapur, Hyderabad – 500049, Telangana. TODO-CONTENT: The school should confirm its designated grievance contact before launch.
